## 🎯 Executive Summary & The Problem

Independent creators, engineering consultants, and boutique digital studios do not fail because of lack of talent — **they fail because of cash flow blindness and payment friction**.

- **The Cash Horizon Trap:** Founders struggle to know whether a delayed $15,000 payment will crash their runway in 30 days or 90 days.
- **The Social Anxiety of Debt Collection:** Chasing overdue invoices feels awkward, unprofessional, and damages long-term client relationships.
- **Unprotected Intellectual Property:** Clients receive production deliverables before final milestone payments clear, leaving studios with zero leverage.
- **Underwriting Blind Spots:** Freelancers take on clients without knowing their historical payment velocity or credit risk.

### The GigPulse Breakthrough

**GigPulse** is an autonomous financial operating system that eliminates financial anxiety for independent operators. By pairing real-time bank telemetry with autonomous agentic intelligence, GigPulse acts as your 24/7 sovereign Chief Financial Officer and collections officer.

## ⚡ 60-Second Judge Quickstart Tour

Follow these steps to evaluate the full GigPulse experience in under 1 minute:

1. **Launch the Local Server:**
   ```bash
   python -m http.server 3000
   ```
2. **Open the Screen Hub:**
   Navigate to [http://localhost:3000/gig_pulse_web_app_screens/](http://localhost:3000/gig_pulse_web_app_screens/) to toggle between mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.
3. **Experience the Landing Page:**
   Visit [http://localhost:3000/gig_pulse_web_app_screens/01_landing_page_full.html](http://localhost:3000/gig_pulse_web_app_screens/01_landing_page_full.html). Hover over the hero image to reveal the Human Founder inside the AI Sentinel.
4. **Use the 1-Click Judge Pass:**
   Click **"LOG IN"** or visit [http://localhost:3000/gig_pulse_web_app_screens/00_auth_login_signup.html](http://localhost:3000/gig_pulse_web_app_screens/00_auth_login_signup.html). Click the amber **"Enter as Alex Mercer ➔"** button.
5. **Test the Autonomous Cockpit:**
   In [02_pulse_cockpit_dashboard.html](http://localhost:3000/gig_pulse_web_app_screens/02_pulse_cockpit_dashboard.html):
   - Click **"Test What-If Scenarios"** in the top action bar to open the top-floating simulation popover.
   - Toggle **"Acme Corp delays $8.5k payment by 30 days"** and watch the runway gauge dynamically contract from 98 days to 54 days!
   - Navigate through the sidebar to explore the Chaser, Scorecard, and Forecaster.

## 🎨 Design System & Aesthetics

GigPulse is built upon our signature **GrowthSphere Luxury Fintech** design language:

- **Canvas Background:** Deep Midnight Navy (`#0A0F1D`) with gradient card containers (`#0E1528` / `#131C35`).
- **Liquid Glassmorphism:** Subtle `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` surface fills, and hairline illuminated borders (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.18)`).
- **Typography:** Modern Google Fonts pairing — `Plus Jakarta Sans` for geometric executive headings, `Inter` for hyper-legible tabular data, and `JetBrains Mono` for cryptographic telemetry.
- **Micro-Interactions:** Custom dual-element trailing cursor, smooth tab transitions, live feedback toasts, and animated status pulses.
